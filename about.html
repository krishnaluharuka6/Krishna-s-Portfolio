Hi...

I'm Krishna Luharuka, an educator, a learner, and an aspiring AI student. I enjoy understanding how things work and, perhaps most importantly, solving problems and finding simple, meaningful ways to explain complex ideas.

As a teacher, I try to make learning feel less overwhelming and more relatable, often using examples, stories, and real-life connections. I believe good teaching is not only about knowing the answer, but helping someone understand why.

I'm still learning, still experimenting, and still figuring out where this journey will take me. But I believe every experience, whether in a classroom, a project, or a difficult problem, has something to teach.

I bring a sense of purpose to my work. When I'm not immersed in it, you'll find me recharging through spirituality or connecting with nature. I believe life is about inner peace and becoming the best version of yourself.
